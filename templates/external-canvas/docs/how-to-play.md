# Canvas Orbit

Collect the moving spark with Space, the west gamepad button or Collect spark. Each collection saves one spark and the first earns a bronze medal. Escape and gamepad Start open MFG's shared pause menu.

On a TV, scan the QR with your phone, enter your name and press Ready. Press Collect spark first to win the round. Use the phone’s pause button to pause the TV, then Resume to play again. After the spark is saved, select Return to lobby on the TV for another round.
