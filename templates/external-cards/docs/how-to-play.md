# Pocket Cards

Choose the card with the largest number. A correct choice earns a win and your first win earns a bronze medal. Save the official cards as a private deck to test your collection. Use Tab and Enter, the gamepad menu controls, or touch. Escape and gamepad Start open MFG's shared pause menu.

To compare cards with a friend, choose Host online room. Give your friend the four-letter code, then have them open the same build and choose Join online room. Each player chooses a card; the higher number wins unless the difference falls within the displayed tie margin. Choose again after a tie. A completed online round saves the winner’s reward and locks the choices. If saving fails, the host can retry. Leave the room to start another round.

Open the pause menu and choose Settings to hide card power or use a compact layout. Card-power preference follows your player in this environment; compact layout stays on this browser.
